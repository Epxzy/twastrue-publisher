Twas True Publisher

Twas True Publisher is a small private tool used by the owner of the history video channel 'Twas True on YouTube. It uploads the channel owner's own finished videos to the owner's own YouTube channel and reads the channel's own public statistics. It is not offered to other people and has no public sign-up.

It uses the YouTube API Services. By using it, the channel owner is bound by the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
